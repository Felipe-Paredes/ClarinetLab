- The batch audit generates 1,248 deterministic studies (13 meters × 4 difficulties × 24 seeds), regenerates each for equality, and validates exact integer measure totals, instrument range and tuplets. Cases vary tonic, mode, length (2–64 bars), tempo, rests, ornaments and tempo expression.

## Implemented
- Shared integer-tick model, seeded motifs/phrase roles, four difficulty levels and exact validation.
- All 13 requested meters, asymmetric grouping, appropriate BPM units and optional phrase-boundary meter changes.
- 15 written tonics, major, three minor forms, chromatic and five additional diatonic modes; contextual enharmonics and double accidentals. Theoretical signatures use explicit accidentals with no signature rather than an incorrect conventional signature.
- Whole through thirty-second rhythms, dotted/double-dotted values, triplets/sixteenth triplets and sextuplets; rests, ties, slurs and breaths.
- ppp–fff, emphasis, hairpins/text, staccatissimo/portato and existing articulations; phrase tempo changes and fermatas.
- Ornament notation and bounded playback expansion; sample-based playback, centralized B-flat transposition and grouped metronome.
- Expandable controls, seed regeneration, register/category/range choices and procedural demonstration button.

## Partial or limited
- Phrase structure, technique targeting and breathing are heuristics, not a conservatory curriculum or a physiological model. Breaths use phrase length/tempo, not a complete density-aware breath budget. Finger-coordination uses pitch patterns, not a full fingering chart. Endurance uses sustained practice length/range rather than a distinct physical-fatigue model.
- Dynamic and articulation effects alter gain, envelopes and note gates. There are no separate recorded velocity/articulation layers or true sampled legato transitions. Ornaments steal host-note time; interpretation is documented approximation, not a universal performance-practice claim.
- Only 3:2 triplets and 6:4 sextuplets are supported. Quintuplets, septuplets and arbitrary nested tuplets are absent.
- Courtesy accidental metadata is renderable; the generator does not implement an exhaustive editorial courtesy policy. Optional modulations use a limited dominant-related plan.
- Dense notation can require horizontal scrolling on narrow screens. Visual music is not yet a fully navigable screen-reader score. The large notation-library bundle remains a performance tradeoff.
- No guided learning, XP, saved library, microphone assessment, account system or other unrelated phase features were added.

## Manual acceptance checklist
1. Open http://127.0.0.1:5175/ and generate at each difficulty; inspect readable treble clef, key, meter and complete measures.
2. Choose Generate V3 demonstration: confirm A major, 32 bars, 6/8 and dotted-quarter = 72. Inspect varied motifs, rests, beams/tuplets, slurs, p–ff, both hairpins and breath marks.
3. Listen with headphones: confirm recognizable clarinet, natural enough attacks/releases and no clicks on long notes. Compare a written C5 with concert B-flat4 on a trusted tuner/instrument.
4. Test Pause → Resume → Stop; change tempo and confirm the notation stays rhythmically unchanged. Enable metronome and check two dotted-quarter pulses per 6/8 bar.
5. Try 7/8 with 2+2+3 and 3+2+2 groups, then 2/2. Confirm grouped accents and displayed beat units.
6. Expand settings; try minor modes, ornaments and musical tempo expression. Listen for the indicated changes without displaced following measures.
7. Generate with the same seed/settings twice, then New variation: the former should match and the latter should change.
8. Test phone width, keyboard navigation and repeated generations; report clipping, hard-to-reach controls or any browser-specific audio failure.
